# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- `Calendar.ListCalendarLogins`, `CreateCalendarLogin`, `RevokeCalendarLogin` and `CreateCalendarLoginProfileLink` manage calendar sign-ins, through `/calendar/logins`. A calendar sign-in belongs to a person, not to an address: its username is `cal-` and ten letters and digits, never an address, and it signs calendar and contacts apps in over CalDAV and CardDAV to that person's calendars, the calendars and address books shared with them and their contacts, even when they hold no address at all. Mail apps refuse it and it never sends. `CreateCalendarLogin` returns the `password`, shown this once, `servers` with the `caldav` and `carddav` servers, and an Apple configuration profile in `appleProfile` with CalDAV and CardDAV only, and `CreateCalendarLoginProfileLink` makes a one-time link that sets one up on an Apple device. Your own take `account:read` and `account:write`, and `nextmail.WithMember` on `ListCalendarLogins`, or `"member"` in the body of the two that create, reaches a member's with `members:write`. Neither of those two is retried.
- `constants.SecurityEventKindsCalendarLoginCreated` (`"calendar-login-created"`) and `constants.SecurityEventKindsCalendarLoginRevoked` (`"calendar-login-revoked"`) are two more kinds in the security log, and the `detail` of an entry carries `login`, the username of the sign-in it is about.
- To-dos: a to-do list is a calendar of kind `todos` (`constants.CalendarKindsTodos`), and its items are RFC 5545 to-dos that calendar apps such as Reminders, Thunderbird and tasks.org sync over CalDAV. `Calendar.ListTodos`, `ListAllTodos` and `IterateTodos` read them through `GET /calendar/todos`, filtered by `nextmail.WithCalendarIDs`, `nextmail.WithState` (`open`, `done` or `all`, the `constants.TodoStates` values), `nextmail.WithDueBefore` and `nextmail.WithDueAfter`. `GetTodo`, `CreateTodo`, `UpdateTodo` and `DeleteTodo` work on one, and `DeleteTodo` answers with how many to-dos it `removed`. A to-do has a `title`, `notes`, a `status` among the `constants.TodoStatuses` values, `start` and `due` as a day or a moment with `allDay` and `timezone`, `completedAt`, `priority`, `percentComplete`, `categories`, the `parentId` of the to-do it is a step of and the `recurrence` it repeats by. Checking off a repeating to-do moves it to its next date. They need `calendar:read` and `calendar:write`, and `CreateTodo` is not retried after a network failure.
- `Calendar.ListCalendars` takes `nextmail.WithKind` and lists your to-do lists with `constants.CalendarKindsTodos`, and `Calendar.CreateCalendar` takes `"kind"` to make one. Members, colours, order and deleting work on to-do lists as they do on calendars.
- `Calendar.DeleteCalendar` takes `nextmail.WithNotifyGuests`: left out or `true`, the guests of every upcoming meeting you organised in the calendar are sent a cancellation, as `CancelEvent` does, and `false` deletes it without telling anyone. The answer carries `cancelledMeetings`. Sending the cancellations needs `emails:send` as well.
- `Threads.ListAutomations` reads which automations ran on a thread and what they did, through `GET /threads/{id}/automations`, with `threads:read` and `automations:read`: a `[]nextmail.Object`, newest first, each with the `automation` it was (`id`, `name` and `status`), its `status` and `trigger`, the case the email matched in `match` (the address or domain that received it, the sender entry and the filters that held, or null), the `messages` of the thread it started from or sent, each with a `role` among the `constants.AutomationThreadRolesValues`, and every step it ran in `steps`, with the step as the version defines it.
- `LiveEvents.Stream` follows the mailbox as it changes, over the server-sent events of `GET /live-events`, with `threads:read`: one `nextmail.LiveEvent` with `ID`, `Type`, `OccurredAt` and `Data` each time a message is filed or sent, a conversation changes or is deleted, or the delivery of a sent email moves on, of the kinds in `constants.LiveEventTypesValues`. It takes `nextmail.WithTypes`, `nextmail.WithAddress` and `nextmail.WithLastEventID`, and returns a `*nextmail.LiveEventStream`: range over its `All()`, or call `Next`, `Event` and `Err`. Leaving the loop of `All()`, `Close` or cancelling the context closes it without an error.
- The stream never ends by itself. It opens again after the server closes it and resumes through `Last-Event-ID`, retries a network failure, a 408, a 429 and a 5xx from a second doubling to 30 seconds, waits as long as `Retry-After` asks up to 5 minutes, and reopens a connection silent for 60 seconds. When the server can no longer replay from where it left off, it delivers an event whose `Type` is `nextmail.LiveEventReset`, with its `reason` in `Data`. A 400, 401, 403, 404 or 422 ends it with the `*nextmail.Error` that `Err` returns.
- `Threads.WaitForEmail` holds the request open until a message that matches arrives and returns it, the way a script waits for a sign-in code. `nextmail.WithFromAddress` and `nextmail.WithSubject` match text in the sender and in the subject, `nextmail.WithAddress` keeps the mail of one address, `nextmail.WithSince` sets the earliest arrival, and `nextmail.WithWaitSeconds` how long to wait, from 0 to 60 seconds and 30 when left out. It answers `message_wait` with the `message_arrival`, or null when nothing arrived in time, and `waitedMs`. The call is given that wait plus 15 seconds, so the client timeout does not cut it short.
- `constants.AddressForwardStatusesBounced` (`"bounced"`) is a forwarding destination whose server refused forwarded mail for good, so nothing is forwarded to it until it confirms again. A route that forwards to one reads `constants.AddressRouteInactiveReasonsForwardBounced` (`"forward_bounced"`). Asking a bounced destination again sends it a new confirmation.
- A traffic entry and a routed message carry `verdicts["arc"]`: the ARC verdict for the servers that forwarded the message on its way in, `pass`, `fail`, `none` or `temperror`, and `nil` on an entry written before ARC was checked.
- `Calendar.ListCalendars`, `GetCalendar`, `CreateCalendar`, `UpdateCalendar` and `DeleteCalendar` manage your calendars: the ones you keep, with your default one among them, and the ones other members of the workspace gave you access to, each with its own `"color"` and `"position"` and a `kind`, one of the `constants.CalendarKinds` values. Deleting a calendar deletes its events, and your default calendar cannot be deleted. `Calendar.SetCalendarMember` and `RemoveCalendarMember` give a member `"read"` or `"write"` access to a calendar you keep, the `constants.CollectionAccessLevels` values, and `access` on a calendar, one of the `constants.CollectionRights` values, says what you may do with it. `SetCalendarMember` is retried after a network failure, since setting the same access twice leaves the same member, and `CreateCalendar` is not.
- Calendar events and occurrences carry `calendarId`. `Calendar.CreateEvent` takes `"calendarId"` to put the event in a calendar and `UpdateEvent` takes it to move one, `ListEvents`, `ListAllEvents` and `IterateEvents` take `nextmail.WithCalendarIDs` to keep only some calendars, `CreateFeed` takes `"calendarId"` to publish one calendar and every feed says which, and `ImportIcs` takes `nextmail.WithCalendarID` for the calendar the events go into, which every import reports.
- `Contacts.ListAddressBooks` lists your address book and the ones other members gave you access to, each with `access`, one of the `constants.CollectionRights` values. `Contacts.SetAddressBookMember` and `RemoveAddressBookMember` give a member `"read"` or `"write"` access to yours, the `constants.CollectionAccessLevels` values, and `SetAddressBookMember` is retried after a network failure. `ListCards`, `ListAllCards` and `IterateCards` take `nextmail.WithAddressBook` to read one you are a member of, and `CreateCard` takes `"addressBook"` to add to one you may write.
- `Traffic.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` read the traffic log: one entry for each message and recipient at each step it took in or out of the workspace, newest first, kept for 30 days. They take `WithDomainID`, `WithDirection`, `WithOutcomes`, `WithAddress`, `WithRecipient`, `WithSince` and `WithUntil`. Outbound entries need `emails:read` and inbound ones `threads:read`.
- `Emails.SendRaw` sends an RFC 5322 message you built yourself, as written: `raw` is its bytes as a `[]byte` or an `io.Reader`, encoded for you, or base64 text, up to 10 MB. The sender is its From header and the recipients its To, Cc and Bcc headers, unless `to`, `cc` or `bcc` give the envelope. It takes the scheduling, tracking, signature, tag and lane options of `Send`, honours `nextmail.WithIdempotencyKey`, and never sends a Bcc header.
- The `email_received` trigger of an automation takes `senders`, the addresses or domains its mail has to come from, where a domain covers its subdomains. Left out or empty, mail from anybody starts it.
- `Settings.Update` takes `labelAlerts`, and `Settings.Get` returns it: label ids under each workspace id whose mail still alerts you while labelled mail stays out of your inbox.
- `MailboxProvidersOpenemail` and `MailServicesOpenemail` (`"openemail"`): the provider of a mailbox on OpenEmail and the `sentWith` of a message sent with it.
- `sentWith` stays empty, rather than naming Outlook or Gmail, when another mail service signed the message and the only hint is that it passed through Microsoft's or Google's servers on the way or was written in the Outlook app.
- `ShareLinks.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` read the templates, forms and automations the workspace shares at a public link, the newest first, with how often each page was opened and each item copied, and take `nextmail.WithKind`, `nextmail.WithLimit` and `nextmail.WithCursor`, where the kind is one of the `constants.ShareKinds` values. `ShareLinks.Get` reads the link of one item, named by its kind and its id, `ShareLinks.Set` makes it public or changes `"copyable"`, and `ShareLinks.Delete` makes it private again. `Set` is retried after a network failure, since an item keeps one link however often it is shared, and `Delete` is not. A private item answers 404 `share_link_not_found`.
- `Shared.Get` reads what the public page of a link shows, from the token at its end and from any workspace, and `Shared.Copy` copies the item into this workspace as a draft. `Copy` is never retried, because a second call makes a second copy. An item its owner shares to look at only answers 403 `share_not_copyable`, and a link that no longer works answers 404 `shared_item_not_found`.
- `Tasks.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` take `nextmail.WithChatID` to read only the tasks made in one assistant chat, the ones the chat shows above its messages, as the Tasks page does when a chat's task is opened. A chat belongs to one person, so the id of a chat you cannot open lists no tasks.

## 0.0.1

The first release of the Go SDK: the module `nextmail.sh`, with the package `nextmail`. It needs Go 1.24 or later and nothing outside the standard library. It has a method for each of the 746 methods of the TypeScript SDK, in the same 53 namespaces and under the same names in Go's casing, as fields of the client: `client.Emails.Send`, `client.Threads.List`, `client.Domains.Verify`. They cover everything from sending and reading mail to contacts, broadcasts, automations, the calendar, booking pages, tasks and the settings of a workspace, and a parity check proves that each one sends the same request as its TypeScript twin.

- `nextmail.New()` reads `NEXTMAIL_API_KEY`, `NEXTMAIL_ACCESS_TOKEN` and `NEXTMAIL_BASE_URL`, and takes `WithAPIKey`, `WithAccessToken`, `WithAccessTokenFunc`, `WithBaseURL`, `WithHTTPClient`, `WithTimeout`, `WithMaxRetries`, `WithUserAgent` and `WithHeader`. It never fails: a missing or malformed credential is reported by `client.Err()` and by the first call.
- Every method takes a `context.Context` first, which cancels the call or gives it a deadline. Request bodies are `nextmail.Body` maps that keep the field names of the API, options are functions such as `nextmail.WithLimit` and `nextmail.WithCursor`, and results are `nextmail.Object` values with readers such as `String`, `Int`, `Bool`, `Time` and `Object`.
- Every paginated resource has `List` for one page, `ListAll` for every item as a slice, and `Iterate`, which fetches the next page only when it gets there. Range over its `All()`, or call `Next`, `Item` and `Err` in a plain loop.
- Failures come back as `*nextmail.Error` values with `Status`, `Type`, `Code`, `Param`, `DocURL`, `RequestID`, `RetryAfter`, `Fields` and `Body`, and `errors.Is` matches them against sentinels such as `ErrNotFound`, `ErrValidation`, `ErrRateLimited`, `ErrStepUpRequired`, `ErrNetwork` and `ErrTimeout`. A mistake in the call itself matches `ErrInvalidArgument` and is returned before anything is sent.
- Reads, sends and every write that is safe to repeat are retried on 408, 500, 502, 503 and 504 with backoff, and on a 429 only when its `Retry-After` is a minute or less. Every send carries an idempotency key that its retries reuse, and `nextmail.WithIdempotencyKey` sets your own.
- An OAuth access token works in place of an API key, given as it is or by a function that runs before every request, so the token can be renewed without a new client. When a change made with a token needs a verification code, the error matches `ErrStepUpRequired`, and `client.Security.BeginStepUp` and `VerifyStepUp` provide one.
- Every workspace is the same kind, the one that holds the free address included. That address sends only the mail a person writes in the app, so a send from it with an API key or an access token is refused with `from_address_forbidden`.
- `nextmail.VerifyWebhookSignature` checks a webhook delivery in constant time, refuses one more than five minutes old and returns the event.
- `nextmail.NewTempMail` builds a client for disposable inboxes that needs no credential, and `client.Request` calls an endpoint no method wraps, with the credential, timeout and retries of the client.
- `nextmail.ResolveLanguage`, `LanguageByCode`, `IsRTLLanguage`, `IsAPIKey`, `IsAccessToken` and `IsSealed` are the helpers of the TypeScript SDK, and the `constants` package holds the sets of values the API uses, such as webhook events, scopes and statuses.
- A client is safe for concurrent use. It sends a credential over plain `http:` only to a server on this machine, its default HTTP client follows no redirects, and printing a client, an option or an error never shows a credential.
- API keys begin `nm_live_` or `nm_test_`, and temporary inbox tokens `nm_inbox_`. The client refuses a key of any other shape.
- `Account.SetPhoto`, `Contacts.SetPhoto`, `Domains.SetAddressPhoto`, `Bookings.UploadBackground`, `Branding.UploadImage` and `Templates.UploadImage` take images up to 100 MB, wait for them as long as `Files.Upload` does, and take `nextmail.WithCrop`: the part of the image to keep, as a `nextmail.ImageCrop`, sent as the `crop` query parameter.
- `Emails.ListEventsByMessageID`, `ListAllEventsByMessageID` and `IterateEventsByMessageID` read the event trail of a sent email found by the Message-ID header it went out with, 1 to 998 characters with or without its angle brackets, for a caller that holds that header and not the `msg_` id, such as a reply or a bounce report that quotes it. They take `nextmail.WithAddress`, `nextmail.WithLimit` and `nextmail.WithCursor` as `ListEvents` does and need `emails:read`, and a Message-ID that no message the key reaches went out with is a 404.
- `ProviderImports.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` take `nextmail.WithSource`, one of the `constants.ProviderImportProviders` values, `nextmail.WithStatus`, one of the `constants.ImportHistoryStatuses` groups, and `nextmail.WithSearch`, up to 320 characters, the filters `Imports.List` takes, sent with every page. An import from a provider never pauses, so `paused` answers an empty page.
- `Emails.ListEvents`, `ListAllEvents` and `IterateEvents` take `nextmail.WithMessageID`, 1 to 998 characters: the trail answers only when the send went out with that Message-ID header, with or without its angle brackets, and is a 404 otherwise. The send id is still required.
- `Files.CreateLink` takes `nextmail.WithReuse`: `true` hands back the working public link the file already has, with its own domain and download count, instead of making another, and a file with none gets a new one. A link handed back answers 200 and a new one 201.
- `Keys.Create`, `Update` and `Delete`, `Keys.Rotate` for any key but the caller, and `Workspaces.OfferOwnership`, `WithdrawOwnershipOffer`, `Account.AcceptOwnershipOffer` and `DeclineOwnershipOffer` refuse an API key with 403 `console_only`, an error that matches `nextmail.ErrPermission`. The console confirms these changes with a verification code, which a key cannot give, so they are made there. A key still rotates its own secret with `Keys.Rotate` or `Me.Rotate`, and revokes keys with `Keys.Revoke`.
- `AppHost.Get`, `Set` and `Verify` report `sender`, where the emails sent to the people of the workspace come from: a map with `address`, `domain` and `waitingOn`. `waitingOn` is `logo` or `signing`, the `constants.AppHostSenderWaits` values, until they come from `noreply@` the domain of the live web app address, and then null. `sender` is null on the free plan and until an address on a verified domain of the workspace is live.
- Every address from `Addresses.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` reports `role`, one of the `constants.AddressRoles` values: `owner` on every address for an API key, and `member` or `viewer` for an app a member connected. A key narrowed to particular addresses or domains lists only the addresses and domains it covers, and the free address of the person the key acts for is listed, with `role` `owner`, by a key of the workspace it belongs to.
- `Chats.ListMessages`, `ListAllMessages` and `IterateMessages` read the messages of a chat, from the first unless `nextmail.WithOrder` is `desc`, one of the `constants.ChatMessageOrders` values. Each message has its `text` and its `parts`, of the kinds in `constants.ChatMessagePartTypes`: the words, the tools the assistant called with their arguments, results and status, one of the `constants.ChatToolCallStatuses` values, and the files. A tool result the caller may not read comes back redacted.
- `Chats.SendMessage` says something in a chat, or starts one under a new id, and returns the chat turn once the assistant has answered. Its `status`, one of the `constants.ChatTurnStatuses` values, is `awaiting_approval` when a call that changes something waits in `pendingToolCalls`, which `Chats.ApproveToolCall`, with optional `"args"`, or `Chats.DenyToolCall` answers, and `"regenerate": true` answers the last message again. `Chats.StopTurn` stops the assistant and answers `stopped` or `idle`, the `constants.ChatStopStatuses` values, and a stopped turn says why in `stopReason`, one of the `constants.ChatStopReasons` values. `"attachments"` takes up to 4 files by `"fileId"` or inline, where bytes in `"content"` are encoded for you. None of the four is retried. The refusals are `chat_busy`, `approval_pending`, `tool_not_offered`, `ai_quota_exceeded`, `ai_not_configured` and `assistant_unavailable`.
- A turn runs for at most 240 seconds, so `Chats.SendMessage`, `ApproveToolCall` and `DenyToolCall` wait at least 5 minutes for it, or the client timeout when that is longer, as uploads wait at least 10. `nextmail.WithTimeout` sets another wait for one call, and zero waits as long as it takes.
- `Members.GetDirectorySync` reads directory sync (SCIM), through which a company directory adds people to the workspace and takes them out again, and `Members.ConnectDirectorySync` turns it on and returns its token once. `Members.DisconnectDirectorySync` turns it off, `Members.ListDirectoryPeople` reads who the directory synced, from 1 with `nextmail.WithStartIndex` and `nextmail.WithCount`, each in one of the `constants.DirectoryPersonStates` states, and `Members.SetDirectoryDefaultRole` and `SetDirectoryGroupRole` choose the roles it gives. They take the role as a `*string`, where nil gives the built-in Member role or no role from the group. Only the owner or a role holding `workspace:manage` reaches them, every change asks an OAuth access token for a verification code, and the refusals are `plan_required`, `role_not_found` and `directory_sync_not_connected`.
- `Security.GetPolicy` reads whether the workspace requires two-factor sign-in and whether the person behind the client meets it, with no scope. `Security.UpdatePolicy` sets `"requireTwoFactor"`, with `members:write` and a verification code.
- `Workspaces.GetOwnershipOffer`, `OfferOwnership` and `WithdrawOwnershipOffer` let the owner offer the workspace to one of its members, and `Account.ListOwnershipOffers`, `AcceptOwnershipOffer` and `DeclineOwnershipOffer` answer the offers made to the person. Offering and accepting ask for a verification code. The refusals are `ownership_offer_not_found`, `ownership_not_eligible`, `ownership_changed`, `domain_moving`, `two_factor_required` and `workspace_allowance_reached`.
- `Account.SetName` changes the name of the person. `Account.ListPushDevices`, `RemovePushDevice` and `SendTestPush` read the phones that get the person's notifications, each with a `platform` from `constants.PushPlatforms`, stop one, and send a test. `Account.EndConnectedAppElevation` makes a connected app ask for verification codes again.
- `DNSConnections.Start` returns the link that connects a Cloudflare account to the workspace in a browser. It asks an OAuth access token for a verification code, and a server that cannot connect a provider answers `dns_unavailable`.
- `Domains.CreateMailAppProfileLink` makes a one-time link that sets an address up on an iPhone, iPad or Mac with a new app password. It needs `members:write` and a verification code, and past 10 links an hour it is `profile_link_rate_limited`. `Domains.StartGravatarPublish` returns the link that publishes the photo of an address to Gravatar, refused with `photo_required` and `gravatar_not_configured`.
- `Threads.SetReplying` and `ClearReplying` show the team who is writing a reply. `Threads.ListTracking` reads the tracking report of every tracked message sent in a thread, and `Threads.GetDelivery` counts what bounced, was refused or was held back, both narrowed by `nextmail.WithAddress`. `Threads.ListFileLinks`, `ListAllFileLinks` and `IterateFileLinks` read the download links one message carried, with `nextmail.WithIncludeRevoked`. `Threads.Counts` takes `nextmail.WithHideLabelled`.
- `Senders.GetAvatar` downloads the picture of a sender as bytes, and `Senders.Get` and `Research` report `avatarUrl` and `avatarKind`, one of the `constants.SenderAvatarKinds` values.
- `Emails.Send` and `SendBatch` take `"isForward"`, `"originalMessage"`, the quoted history of a forward or a reply, and `"encryption"`, a map with `"format": "pgp-mime"`, `"sealedAt"` and `"sealedTo"` for a message you sealed yourself with OpenPGP. `Emails.Translate` takes `"skipQuoted"` and reports `quotedHistoryKept`. An attachment of `Emails.Send` can also be a map with `"url"` and optionally `"filename"` and `"contentType"`, a file the server fetches once.
- `Emails.ListEvents`, `ListAllEvents`, `IterateEvents` and `GetTracking`, and the opens and clicks methods of `Tracking`, take `nextmail.WithAddress`. `Tracking.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` take `nextmail.WithOffsetMinutes`, and `Tracking.GetStats` reports `downloads`, the files that went out as download links and how often they were fetched.
- `Templates.Update` and `ReplaceContent` take `"expectedUpdatedAt"`, and `Templates.Publish` takes `nextmail.WithExpectedUpdatedAt`: the `updatedAt` of the version you read, which refuses a change saved in between with 409 `version_conflict`. Every template version reports `updatedAt`.
- `Drafts.Create` and `Update` take `"attachments"`, up to 20 files inline or by `"fileId"`, where bytes in `"content"` are encoded for you, and `Drafts.Get` reports the `fileId` of each file.
- Filters: `nextmail.WithRuleIDs` on `Rules.ListRuns`, `ListAllRuns` and `IterateRuns`, `nextmail.WithAudienceID` on `Contacts.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate`, `nextmail.WithFrom` and `nextmail.WithTo` on `Contacts.Activity`, `nextmail.WithInactiveDays` on `Audiences.ListContacts`, `ListAllContacts` and `IterateContacts`, `nextmail.WithSince` and `nextmail.WithUntil` on `Audiences.Growth`, `Forms.Analytics` and `Bookings.Analytics`, `nextmail.WithSince` on `Broadcasts.Analytics` and `Broadcasts.Stats`, `nextmail.WithSource`, `nextmail.WithStatus`, one of the `constants.ImportHistoryStatuses` groups, and `nextmail.WithSearch` on `Imports.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate`, and `nextmail.WithFromAddress` on `Signatures.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate`.
- `Contacts.SetAudiences` and `Audiences.AddContact` take `"createContact": true`, which saves an address that is not a contact yet as one first and then needs `contacts:write`. Through an app a member connected, an address another member saved is a 409 `contact_exists` there and on `Contacts.Save`.
- Scopes: `Addresses.List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` need `emails:read`, or `emails:send` in its place. `Audiences.ListContacts`, `ListAllContacts` and `IterateContacts` need `contacts:read` as well as `audiences:read`. `Emails.Compose`, `Rewrite`, `SuggestSubject` and `Translate` take `drafts:write` in place of `emails:send`. `Domains.GetMailAppSettings` and `ListAppPasswords` need `threads:read`, and `Domains.UpdateMailAppSettings` needs `threads:read` and `domains:write`. `Domains.CreateAddress` asks an OAuth access token for a verification code when it chooses where the mail goes or deletion on expiry.
- `Domains.CreateAddress` and `Domains.UpdateAddress` take `"expiresAt"` and `"onExpiry"`, one of the `constants.AddressExpiryActions` values, and every address reports them with `expiredAt`. From its `expiresAt` an address takes no mail and sends none, a send from it is refused with 403 `address_expired`, and within a minute it is switched off, or deleted with the conversations delivered to it when `onExpiry` is `delete`. A later `expiresAt`, nil or `"enabled": true` turns a stopped address back on. `constants.WebhookEventsAddressExpired` is the event `address.expired`.
- The `constants.DomainAddressDestinations` values gain `webhook` and `group`. The same two methods take `"webhookId"` for a webhook destination, `"tapWebhookIds"` to copy every message to up to 5 endpoints, and `"groupPosting"`, one of the `constants.AddressGroupPostingPolicies` values, and `"groupKeepCopy"` for a group, and every address reports them. The refusals are `webhook_not_found` and `too_many_taps`.
- `constants.WebhookEventsEmailRouted` is the event `email.routed`: a message that arrived at an address whose destination is `webhook`, or that a route of its domain sent to a webhook, was kept for 30 days instead of being filed. It goes only to the endpoint the address or route names and to the taps of the address, and always carries `content` and `raw`. `routedBy` says which sent it, with its `kind` one of the `constants.WebhookRoutedSources` values, `recipient` keeps the plus tag and `verdicts` are what the receiving server found. An `email.received` event carries `tap`, true when an endpoint gets the message as a tap of the address rather than through its subscription.
- `Domains.ListRoutes`, `ListAllRoutes` and `IterateRoutes` read the pattern routes of a domain in the order they are tried, and `Domains.CreateRoute`, `GetRoute`, `UpdateRoute` and `DeleteRoute` manage them: a route catches mail to addresses nobody created whose local part matches its `pattern`, such as `support-*`, and delivers it to an address, forwards it once the destination confirms, posts it to a webhook as `email.routed` or reads it on another domain, as the `constants.AddressRouteActions` values say. `active` and `inactiveReason`, one of the `constants.AddressRouteInactiveReasons` values, say whether a route takes mail now. A domain holds at most 100 routes, and the refusals are `invalid_route_pattern`, `route_pattern_taken`, `route_target_not_found` and `route_limit_reached`. `Domains.TestRoute` answers which step decides mail to an address without sending anything, one of the `constants.AddressRouteTestSteps` values, with the route that decided and where the mail would end up.
- `Domains.ListGroupMembers`, `ListAllGroupMembers` and `IterateGroupMembers` read the group members of a group address, and `Domains.AddGroupMembers` and `RemoveGroupMember` change them. A group member is an address of the workspace or an address at another provider, which confirms by email first, as the `constants.GroupMemberKinds` values say. A group holds at most 1,000 group members and groups nest at most 3 deep, and the refusals are `not_a_group`, `group_member_limit`, `group_member_not_found` and `group_nesting_too_deep`.
- `Domains.Update` takes `"aliasOf"`, which makes the domain an alias of another domain of the workspace, and every domain reports `aliasOf` and `aliases`. The refusals are `alias_not_allowed`, `alias_chain` and `alias_has_addresses`, and creating an address on an alias is refused with `alias_has_addresses`.
- `Domains.UpdateAddressLimits` takes `receiving.storageBytes`, a storage quota for one address from 1 MiB to 1 TiB, and `Domains.GetAddressLimits` reports it. A message that would take the address past its quota gets a permanent bounce (5.2.2), and deleting mail for good makes room again. `Domains.GetAddressLimits` also reports what the address keeps in `usage.storage`, with `bytes` and `messages`: every message filed in its mailbox since storage counting began, with its kept original when it keeps originals. Mail stored before then and mail routed to a webhook are not counted.
- `Webhooks.ListDeadLetters`, `ListAllDeadLetters` and `IterateDeadLetters` read the events an endpoint gave up on: each event whose last automatic attempt failed, with how many times it was tried, the last answer and that attempt. `nextmail.WithStatus` reads the `open` ones by default, or `replayed`, `discarded` or `all`, the `constants.WebhookDeadLetterFilters` values. A dead letter is kept 30 days.
- `Webhooks.ReplayDeadLetters` sends up to 100 open dead letters again in one call, the named ones or the oldest, and answers with an `outcome` for each, one of the `constants.WebhookDeadLetterReplayOutcomes` values. A delivered one is closed as `replayed`. `Webhooks.DiscardDeadLetter` closes one without sending it, and one already replayed is 409 `dead_letter_resolved`.
- `Webhooks.Create` and `Webhooks.Update` take `"retryPolicy"`, one of the `constants.WebhookRetryPolicies` values: `standard` tries a failed delivery up to 8 times over about 27 and a half hours, and `extended` up to 16 times over about 3 days. They also take `"includeRaw": true`, which needs `threads:read`, and every endpoint reports both.
- `Threads.GetRawMessage` downloads the source of one message as `message/rfc822` bytes, ready to save as an `.eml` file. It is the message byte for byte as it arrived when the address had `keepOriginal` on, and otherwise a source rebuilt from the stored message, the same bytes the IMAP server gives a mail app. The `X-Message-Source` response header says which, one of the `constants.RawMessageSources` values.
- `Domains.CreateAddress` and `Domains.UpdateAddress` take `"keepOriginal": true`, and every address reports it. While it is on, each message the address receives is also kept byte for byte as it arrived, and it is deleted with its message.
- An `email.received` event to an endpoint with `includeRaw` on carries `data.raw`: a link that downloads the message source for an hour, its `size` and its `source`, `original` or `rebuilt`.
- `Domains.GetAddressLimits` and `Domains.UpdateAddressLimits` read and change what one address may send and receive: a hold that pauses or disables sending or receiving, caps per hour, per 24 hours and per month on messages and recipients, recipients per message, and lists of allowed and blocked recipients and allowed senders, with the usage so far. The `constants.AddressHolds` values list the holds.
- `Domains.Get` and `Domains.Update` give each DNS record `found`, what public DNS answers at that name now, and `problem`, with a `code` from the `constants.DomainRecordProblems` values and a `message` to show a person.
- `Domains.Update` takes `"autodiscovery": true`, which offers the SRV and TXT records that let mail, calendar and contacts apps find the servers from an address on the domain alone, listed in `autodiscoveryRecords`.
- `Webhooks.Create` and `Webhooks.Update` take `"includeContent": true`, which puts the message into every `email.received` event as `data.content`: the text, the HTML up to 512 KiB and each attachment with a link that downloads it for an hour. Turning it on needs `threads:read`.
- A connected mailbox can be the sender of `Emails.Send` with an API key, of broadcasts, automations and sign-up forms, and a key or an app can send from the free address of the person it acts for. Only that person may send as a free address.
- `Threads.Counts` leaves labelled mail out of `team.assigned` and `team.unassigned`, as the inbox does, unless the person has chosen to show labelled mail in the inbox.
- `List`, `ListAll` and `Iterate` of `Domains`, `Members`, `Keys`, `Webhooks` and `Rules`, and `ConnectedMailboxes.List`, take `nextmail.WithQ`, which searches every field a person reads on the row: names, addresses, ids, scopes, events, condition values. Every word has to appear, ignoring case and accents, and close spellings count when nothing matches exactly.
- `Rules.Stats` reads how the rules did inside a window: messages matched and failed, a series of buckets, the matches of each rule, the actions taken, the failures and the senders matched most. `nextmail.WithRuleIDs` counts some rules only.
- `Billing.Get` reports `usage.addressChecks`, the deep address checks used this calendar month against what the plan includes, and `Billing.ListPlans` each plan's `monthlyAddressChecks`. `Tools.CheckAddress` and `Tools.CheckAddresses` with the deep check first use the deep checks the plan includes each month (10 on Free, 100 on Starter, 500 on Business, 1,000 on Enterprise), then pay as you go at $1.50 per 100 addresses.
- An automation branch can check the contact's address: `{"kind": "address_check", "mailbox": ..., "accept": ...}`, with `accept` from `constants.AutomationAddressAcceptsValues()`.
- A send leaves out an address whose domain takes no mail, and one the check on sending finds has no mailbox. Each shows as `suppressed`, with an `email.suppressed` webhook that names the reason, never as a bounce.
- Forms check the address of every sign-up: `Forms.Subscribe` refuses one whose domain takes no mail with the field error `undeliverable`. `refuseDisposable` in the settings also refuses throwaway addresses (`disposable`), `checkMailbox` runs a deep check on every sign-up and refuses a missing mailbox (`mailbox_not_found`), and a refused address that looks like a typo carries `suggestion`.
